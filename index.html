Уровень A1
  • Глагол to be: am / is / are
  • Subject pronouns: I, you, he, she, it, we, they
  • Артикли в английском: a / an / the
  • Plural nouns: -s, -es и исключения
  • This, that, these, those
  • Притяжательные прилагательные в английском: my, your, his, her, its, our, their
Уровень A2
  • Comparative adjectives: bigger, more interesting
  • Superlative adjectives: the biggest, the most interesting
  • Наречия образа действия в английском: quickly, well
  • Will: прогнозы и быстрые решения
  • Present Perfect для опыта: Have you ever...?
  • Present Perfect с just, already, yet
Уровень B1
  • Second conditional: If I had more time, I would travel
  • Passive voice: present and past
  • Reported speech: statements
  • Present perfect continuous: I've been waiting
  • Past perfect (had done)
  • Zero conditional: если нагреть лёд, он тает
